Arguably the most difficult conventional geometry unit. Like all summit units, there are no lecture notes.
There are 4 categories named after the 4 periods of classical music, but the problems aren't in strict
chronological order. It appears that the classical problems are mostly intended to be, well, classical, and the contemporary problems rather modern in style.

(evan please delete this line if you disagree) The unit teaches some niche theory but is otherwise just 
miscellaneous hard geo.

# Notable problems
`20USATST6`: Probably the most difficult geometry problem to appear on an national or international contest ever.
`21SLG8`: A great lesson in common geo heuristics but not elementarily doable.
`14SLG6`: This showcases a different geo heuristic but equally valuable.
